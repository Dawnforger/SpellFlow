# Grave Fiend Test Plan (Skeleton)

1. Confirm glyph grants passive aura.
2. Confirm max Runic Power increases to 200.
3. At 200 RP, confirm overload aura applies once.
4. Confirm RP drain happens every second and increases each tick.
5. Confirm RP-generators receive:
   - +75% damage
   - +75% RP generation
6. Confirm basic attacks restore random depleted rune with 1s ICD.
7. Confirm rune spender GCD is reduced by 50% only during overload.
8. Confirm overload ends at <= 40 RP and all bonuses stop.
9. Edge cases: relog, death, zone transfer, PvP interactions.
